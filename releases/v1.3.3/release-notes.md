# Tabatha 1.3.3

_Adds to 1.3.2: a toolbar with sort, stars, collapsible sections and per-card preview capture (8 Oct); quick actions, a side panel with saved groups and bookmarks, Russian and Spanish, the "use Alt+Q" note, a two-step "How is Tabatha working for you?" with a Pro waitlist button, the tab count on the icon and an uninstall page (11 Oct). One new **optional** permission (`bookmarks`); no new required one. The privacy policy changed (PRIVACY.md, 11 October 2026)._

## What changed (12 Oct, from the review)
- **Look:** one warm amber accent instead of lime; windows moved to a **sidebar on the left**, each with a picture of the tab it shows (chips removed; Alt+W still cycles); "Tabatha Pro · Join the waitlist"; Y-bit peeks from behind the Pro/coffee buttons once a day and on hover.
- **Quick actions, select first:** nothing acts until tabs are ticked; while searching, "Select all N matching tabs"; the bar is a floating pill that never moves the cards; plain labels and tooltips (incl. what "Copy as Markdown" is for); **Group** asks for a name (the search word to start with) and a colour.
- **Duplicates shown first:** "N duplicates" shows only the copies, each marked Stays / Closes, then "Close N copies" (second click confirms). Only extra copies close.
- **Tab groups in the panel:** open and saved groups, folded by default, drag to reorder (open groups move in Chrome's tab strip too); a ⋯ menu on each (also on group headers): Rename or recolour, Select its tabs, Bookmark the group, Move to a new window, Put its tabs to sleep, Ungroup, Close (keep it saved), Delete.
- **Bookmarks:** a ⋯ menu on folders (Rename, New folder, Bookmark the ticked tabs here, Delete folder, which says how many bookmarks are inside); drag bookmarks and folders to reorder or move them; Cancel on a new folder removes it.
- **Tour:** each step sits next to what it explains, with an arrow; new steps for windows (when there are several), the panel and Settings; the search shortcut (typing anywhere, Ctrl/⌘+F).
- **Settings (⚙ in the footer):** Tabatha's own language choice (Auto / English / Русский / Español), so anyone can switch back; "Open Tabatha when Chrome starts" (off by default); a link to change the Alt+Q shortcut.
- **When things don't work:** "Capture" asks the worker first ("Nothing to capture" instead of closing for nothing), shows a clear progress card with Stop, and reopens Tabatha afterwards; pages Chrome never lets extensions capture (the Web Store) get no Capture button, and a refused capture says so; if Alt+Q is unassigned Tabatha says so with the fix; opening from the toolbar icon shows the shortcut.
- **Fixes:** no more flash on every redraw; the page behind no longer scrolls; filters and sorts jump to the top; cut-off text has a tooltip; the panel no longer changes width; **Sleep** marks the card at once and unloads the tab when you leave Tabatha (the card no longer vanishes); the bookmarks bar starts open.

## What changed (11 Oct)
- **Quick actions.** While searching, a bar shows how many tabs matched and acts on all of them: Close (two presses for more than one), Copy links, Copy as Markdown, Bookmark (several go into a folder named after the search), New window, Group (named after the search when tab groups are on), Sleep, Pin/Unpin, Mute/Unmute. Tick tabs instead with the box on a card, Ctrl/Cmd+click, or Shift+click for a range; "Select all N" and "Clear"; Esc clears the selection. Each card's preview has hover buttons: copy link, bookmark, sleep.
- **Close duplicates.** "N duplicates · Close" in the toolbar keeps one tab per page (pinned first, else the most recently used) and closes the rest, after a second click.
- **Side panel**, like a layers panel: **Saved groups** (closed tab groups moved here from the bottom of the switcher; unfold one to see its tabs and open just one, click the name to reopen the group, × twice to remove) and **Bookmarks** (optional permission: browse folders, search with the main search box, open a bookmark or switch to its open tab, drag tab cards onto a folder, rename, edit the address, move to another folder, new folder, "open and remove from bookmarks", delete with a second click). Opens by itself on wide screens until closed; "Groups & bookmarks" in the toolbar toggles it.
- **Languages.** The whole interface (switcher, tour, dialogs, the notes on web pages, the capture window, the extension's name and description) is in English, Russian and Spanish, following Chrome's language. Russian plurals are handled ("1 вкладка, 2 вкладки, 5 вкладок"). Times use the language's own format.
- **"Use Alt+Q" note.** Chrome doesn't tell extensions about Ctrl+Tab, so Tabatha watches for several quick switches to the next or previous tab. The second time that happens in a day, once the switching stops, a small note on the page says Alt+Q shows every tab at once ("Try it", "Got it", "Don't show again"). At most once a day and three times ever, never within 10 minutes of using Tabatha, never during the first-run notice, and never again after 30 opens.
- **"How is Tabatha working for you?"** replaces the quiet review banner: after 3 days and 15 opens (was 40 opens and 10 days, after three banners), never on the first open of a day, at most twice, 14 days apart. "Love it" offers the store review; "It's okay" / "Needs work" offer "Tell me what would help" (the site's survey, with the answer filled in) and still the review. Both show a lime **Join the Tabatha Pro waitlist** button. "Don't ask again" ends it. The answer is not stored or sent.
- **Pro waitlist button** (lime, next to the coffee button, after the tour has been seen) opens the site's /pro page. One switch: `SHOW_PRO` in `lib/constants.js`.
- **Uninstall page.** Chrome now opens the site's /uninstall page after someone removes Tabatha (one optional question, in their language).
- **Tab count** on the toolbar icon.
- **"Closed in the last 7 days"** heading while searching, so the 7-day reach is visible.
- Links Tabatha opens on the site carry the interface language (`&lang=en|ru|es`) besides the source and version; nothing typed by the user ever goes in a link.

## What changed (8 Oct)
- **Sort, stars, collapsible sections, and capturing one tab.** A toolbar under the search box holds the window chips, a **Starred** filter and a **sort** control: Recent (default, like Alt+Tab), Newest, Oldest (by when the tab was opened, as far as Chrome's tab ids tell), A–Z, and **By site** (one section per site with two or more tabs, biggest first, the rest under "Other sites"). Alt+Q always cycles by recent use, whatever the sort. Every section header (tab groups, sites, "Ungrouped") **collapses** with a click; Tabatha remembers it, and if Alt+Q lands on a tab inside a collapsed section, that section opens for that visit only. **Stars** (☆ on a card, or Alt+S / ⌥S) mark pages inside Tabatha, not Chrome's bookmarks; the Starred filter shows starred open tabs plus "Starred, not open" pages to reopen, and a starred page keeps its preview. **"Turn on tab groups"** and **capturing previews** moved out of the footer into that toolbar. Cards without a preview have a **Capture preview** button (or Alt+P / ⌥P) that shows just that tab for a moment and comes back (if the tab is in the same window, the switcher closes and reopens where it was); the toolbar's "N without preview · Capture" button now captures only the tabs the current view shows (a window, a search, the Starred filter), up to 100 at a time.
- **Plainer preview messages.** The yellow warning that quoted Chrome's error ("Extension has not been invoked for the current page...") is now a quiet grey note in plain words, with an "Allow on all sites" link when that is the fix. Chrome's own text is only in the tooltip. Messages about Chrome's rate limit are not shown.
